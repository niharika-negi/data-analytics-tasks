# Types of Data Analytics

## 1. Introduction

Data Analytics can be divided into four main types:

1. Descriptive Analytics
2. Diagnostic Analytics
3. Predictive Analytics
4. Prescriptive Analytics

These types help organizations understand past
events, identify reasons, predict future outcomes
and decide what actions can be taken.

---

## 2. Descriptive Analytics

Descriptive Analytics answers:

> **What happened?**

It summarizes historical data using reports,
dashboards, charts and tables.

### Examples

- Total sales in a month
- Number of customers
- Monthly revenue
- Number of products sold

### Example

An online store had sales of ₹5,00,000 in August.

Descriptive Analytics reports this historical
sales information.

### Common Techniques

- Reports
- Dashboards
- Charts
- Tables
- Aggregations

---

## 3. Diagnostic Analytics

Diagnostic Analytics answers:

> **Why did it happen?**

It examines data to identify the reasons behind
a particular result.

### Examples

- Why did sales decrease?
- Why did customer complaints increase?
- Why did website traffic decrease?

### Example

An online store's sales decreased in August.

Further analysis shows that one major product
was out of stock for several days.

This helps explain the reason behind the decrease.

### Common Techniques

- Filtering
- Drill-down analysis
- Comparison
- Correlation analysis
- Root-cause analysis

---

## 4. Predictive Analytics

Predictive Analytics answers:

> **What is likely to happen?**

It uses historical data, patterns and statistical
or machine learning techniques to estimate future
outcomes.

### Examples

- Predicting future sales
- Predicting customer churn
- Predicting product demand
- Predicting future revenue

### Example

An e-commerce company uses previous sales data
to estimate next month's sales.

### Common Techniques

- Regression
- Machine Learning
- Time-series analysis
- Predictive models

---

## 5. Prescriptive Analytics

Prescriptive Analytics answers:

> **What should we do?**

It uses data and predictions to suggest possible
actions or decisions.

### Examples

- What price should a company set?
- How much inventory should be maintained?
- Which marketing strategy should be used?
- Which action may improve sales?

### Example

If predicted demand for a product is high,
prescriptive analysis may suggest increasing
inventory before the expected increase in demand.

### Common Techniques

- Optimization
- Simulation
- Decision analysis
- Scenario analysis

---

## 6. Comparison of the Four Types

| Type | Main Question | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Descriptive | What happened? | Summarize past data | Last month's sales |
| Diagnostic | Why did it happen? | Find reasons | Reason for sales decrease |
| Predictive | What may happen? | Estimate future outcomes | Next month's sales |
| Prescriptive | What should we do? | Suggest actions | Decide inventory level |

---

## 7. Simple Business Example

Consider an e-commerce company.

### Descriptive

Sales were ₹10 lakh in January.

**Question:** What happened?

### Diagnostic

Sales decreased by 15% in February because
several products were unavailable.

**Question:** Why did it happen?

### Predictive

Based on historical sales, March sales are
estimated to increase.

**Question:** What may happen?

### Prescriptive

The company should increase inventory for
high-demand products.

**Question:** What should we do?

---

## 8. Analytics Flow

The four types can be understood as:

**What happened?**
↓
Descriptive Analytics

**Why did it happen?**
↓
Diagnostic Analytics

**What may happen?**
↓
Predictive Analytics

**What should we do?**
↓
Prescriptive Analytics

---

## 9. Key Takeaway

The four types of Data Analytics provide different
levels of understanding:

- Descriptive Analytics explains past events.
- Diagnostic Analytics identifies possible reasons.
- Predictive Analytics estimates future outcomes.
- Prescriptive Analytics supports decisions about
  possible actions.
