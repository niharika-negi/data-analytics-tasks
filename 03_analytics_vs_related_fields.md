# Analytics vs Related Fields

## 1. Introduction

Data Analytics, Data Science and Business Analytics
are related fields, but they have different purposes,
roles and areas of focus.

---

## 2. Data Analytics

Data Analytics focuses on examining existing data
to find useful information, patterns and trends.

### Common Activities

- Data cleaning
- Data analysis
- Data visualization
- Reporting
- Finding trends
- Generating insights

### Common Tools

- Excel
- SQL
- Power BI
- Tableau
- Python

### Example

A Data Analyst may analyze monthly sales data to find
which products and regions are performing well.

---

## 3. Data Science

Data Science is a broader field that combines data
analysis, statistics, programming and machine learning
to solve complex problems.

### Common Activities

- Data analysis
- Machine learning
- Predictive modeling
- Feature engineering
- Model evaluation
- Working with large datasets

### Common Tools

- Python
- R
- SQL
- Jupyter Notebook
- Machine Learning libraries

### Example

A Data Scientist may build a machine learning model
to predict future customer purchases.

---

## 4. Business Analytics

Business Analytics focuses on using data to support
business decisions and improve business performance.

### Common Activities

- Business reporting
- KPI analysis
- Performance analysis
- Sales analysis
- Customer analysis
- Business decision support

### Common Tools

- Excel
- SQL
- Power BI
- Tableau

### Example

A Business Analyst may analyze sales and customer
data to help management decide which products or
markets should receive more attention.

---

## 5. Comparison

| Aspect | Data Analytics | Data Science | Business Analytics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main Focus | Analyze data and find insights | Build models and solve complex data problems | Support business decisions |
| Main Goal | Understand what happened and why | Predict or model outcomes | Improve business performance |
| Common Work | Cleaning, analysis, visualization | ML, modeling, analysis | Reporting, KPIs, business analysis |
| Tools | Excel, SQL, Power BI, Python | Python, SQL, ML libraries | Excel, SQL, Power BI |
| Output | Insights and reports | Models and predictions | Business recommendations and reports |
| Example | Analyze monthly sales | Predict future sales | Analyze business performance |

---

## 6. Simple Example

Consider an online shopping company.

### Data Analyst

Analyzes previous sales data and creates a dashboard
showing sales by month, product and region.

### Data Scientist

Uses historical customer data to build a model that
predicts which customers may purchase a product.

### Business Analyst

Uses sales and customer insights to support business
decisions related to products, markets and performance.

---

## 7. Relationship Between the Fields

These fields can work together.

A simplified workflow can be:

**Data → Data Analysis → Insights → Models/Predictions → Business Decisions**

Different roles may contribute at different stages
depending on the organization and project.

---

## 8. Key Takeaway

Data Analytics focuses mainly on analyzing data and
communicating insights.

Data Science combines data analysis with programming,
statistics and machine learning to solve broader and
more complex problems.

Business Analytics focuses on applying data and
insights to business problems and decision-making.
